Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 16, 2026

Gallardo Luxe LLC, doing business as Veteran Home Acquisitions ("Veteran Home Acquisitions," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website at veteranhomeacquisitions.com (the "Site"), by phone, and through our SMS text messaging program, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Contact information, such as your first and last name, phone number, and email address;
  • Property information, such as the property address and its condition;
  • Communications you send us by phone, text message, email, or through forms on the Site;
  • Basic technical information collected automatically when you visit the Site, such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your property inquiries and prepare cash offers;
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry, appointments, offers, and transactions;
  • Send SMS text messages you have consented to receive (see Section 3);
  • Improve our Site and services;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS Text Messaging Program

When you check the consent box on our web form or otherwise provide written consent, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Gallardo Luxe LLC d/b/a Veteran Home Acquisitions related to your property inquiry, including property inquiry responses, cash offer updates, appointment reminders, transaction updates, and follow-up messages.

  • Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and transaction status.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by your mobile carrier.
  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • For help, reply HELP to any message, email us at info@veteranhomeacquisitions.com, or call (866) 569-9117.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or of receiving any service from us.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. All other categories of personal information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; that information will not be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party under any circumstances.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share personal information (excluding SMS opt-in data and consent, which is never shared) only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as title companies, escrow agents, and closing attorneys, solely as needed to complete a transaction you have requested;
  • When required by law, subpoena, or other legal process;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Veteran Home Acquisitions, our customers, or others.

5. Data Security & Retention

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the information below.
  • You may opt out of email communications by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
