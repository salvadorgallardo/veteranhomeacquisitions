FAQ's

How fast can you close on my house?

We can close in as little as 7 days. Because Veteran Home Acquisitions buys with cash, there's no loan underwriting, appraisal, or inspection holding things up the way a traditional sale would. We manage the paperwork and logistics so you sell on your timeline, not the bank's.

Do I need to make repairs to sell my house?

No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. Roof problems, foundation issues, plumbing, structural damage, none of it stops us, and you won't spend anything on renovations. No cleaning or staging needed either; we take care of all repairs after closing.

What are your fees and commissions?

None. Veteran Home Acquisitions charges no realtor commissions, no closing costs, and no hidden fees. A traditional sale can eat up 6 to 10% in commissions plus closing expenses. With us, the price we quote is the amount you walk away with.

How do you determine your cash offer?

We look at your property's current condition, location, market trends, and comparable sales nearby, then base our offer on what we can invest in the home after repairs and resale. You'll have your offer within 24 hours of sending us your property details.
